[16] Small-Sample Time-Series Forecasting in Energy Economics

[16] 能源经济中的小样本时间序列预测研究

National国家级 Completed结项 PI负责人  ·  2024 – 2025  ·  ZHGC54714

Background: Energy-economics series such as national renewable-energy and natural-gas consumption offer only a handful of annual observations, where conventional statistical and machine-learning forecasters overfit. Grey models are built for exactly this small-sample regime, and the kernel-regularized non-homogeneous grey model (KRNGM, Ma et al. 2017) is a strong representative.
Method: Lead-authored the proposal and coordinated execution; re-engineered KRNGM via conformable fractional accumulation (CFA) into CFAKRNGM, tuned the accumulation order with the Hiking Optimization Algorithm (HOA), and benchmarked against KRNGM on Chinese renewable-energy and natural-gas consumption, scored by MAPE and ARE.
Innovation: 1) replaces the integer-order accumulation of KRNGM with a conformable fractional operator, making the accumulation order a learnable parameter rather than a fixed choice; 2) couples that order with kernel regularization so smoothing strength and accumulation order are optimized jointly; 3) delegates order search to HOA instead of a manual grid, making the procedure reproducible.
Outcome: CFAKRNGM matches the KRNGM baseline on both cases, but the optimum falls back to integer-order accumulation (alpha = 0.9998 and 0.9995), i.e. the data did not activate the fractional mechanism the model was built around.
Significance: A negative result worth reporting: it delimits when fractional-order accumulation is worth its extra complexity, and it is the case that first taught me to check whether a proposed mechanism is actually exercised by the data rather than only assumed.

背景：可再生能源消费、天然气消费这类能源经济序列每年只有个位数的观测点，常规统计与机器学习预测器在这种样本量下极易过拟合。灰色模型正是为小样本情形设计的，其中核正则化非齐次灰色模型（KRNGM，Ma et al. 2017）是有代表性的一支。
方法：主笔撰写申报书并统筹项目执行；以共形分数阶累加（CFA）改造 KRNGM，构建 CFAKRNGM，用 Hiking 优化算法（HOA）搜索累加阶数，在中国可再生能源消费与天然气消费两组小样本时序上与 KRNGM 基线对比，以 MAPE 与 ARE 评估精度。
创新点：1) 以共形分数阶算子替换 KRNGM 的整数阶累加，把累加阶数从固定设定变为可学习参数；2) 将该阶数与核正则化耦合优化，使平滑强度与累加阶数联合确定；3) 用 HOA 替代人工网格搜索阶数，使调参过程可复现。
结论：CFAKRNGM 在两个算例上与 KRNGM 基线相当，但搜出的最优阶数分别为 α = 0.9998 与 α = 0.9995，即最优解退回了整数阶累加——模型赖以立论的分数阶机制并未被数据激活。
意义：这是一个值得如实报告的负结果：它划出了分数阶累加"多付出的复杂度"在什么条件下并不划算，也是我第一次意识到，必须检验所提机制是否真的被数据用到，而不能只停留在假设。

[15] Multimodal High-Dimensional Statistical Modeling and Causal Inference for Cotton-Yield Estimation and Climate-Risk Early Warning in Xinjiang

[15] 新疆棉花产量估计与气候风险预警的多模态高维统计建模及因果推断研究

National国家级 Key Program重点项目 Under Application申请中 Participant参与  ·  2026 – Present今

Background: Xinjiang grows over 90% of China's cotton, yet extreme heat, drought, cold spells and hail make yields swing year to year. Remote sensing, reanalysis meteorology and agronomic panels now form a high-dimensional, multimodal, spatio-temporal data system without matching inference methods.
Method: Builds a joint factor model over remote-sensing tensors, meteorological series and agronomic panels, fused by group sparsity, low-rank tensor completion and joint-individual variance decomposition (solved by ADMM and block coordinate descent); estimates climate-to-yield causal effects on the low-dimensional factors via spatio-temporal doubly-debiased Lasso with causal forests; and issues online warnings through multi-stream sparse CUSUM / INSPECT with PCMCI.
Innovation: 1) a heterogeneous multimodal tensor-sparse fusion framework, with a non-asymptotic convergence rate and selection consistency to be proved; 2) joint spatio-temporal doubly-debiased Lasso and causal-forest estimation, targeting root-NT asymptotic normality; 3) multi-stream online high-dimensional change-point detection coupled to causal attribution, targeting ARL/EDD bounds.
Status: Under application (filed June 2026, two-year horizon). No experimental results yet. The planned empirical grain is roughly 1,305 county-year samples across 87 counties in 14 prefectures of Xinjiang, 2010–2024. I drafted the proposal and the accompanying survey.
Significance: Aims to fill the gap between high-dimensional multimodal inference and cotton yield / climate-risk warning. Honest boundary: everything above is planned rather than done, the three theorems are targets not results, and the data agreements with the meteorological and statistical bureaus are at preliminary-intent stage only.

背景： 新疆棉花产量占全国 90% 以上，但极端高温、干旱、寒潮、冰雹频发使产量年际波动加剧。遥感、再分析气象与农情面板已构成"高维 × 多模态 × 时空"的数据体系，却缺乏配套的高维多模态统计推断方法。
方法： 构建遥感张量、气象时序与农情面板的联合因子模型，以群组稀疏 × 低秩张量补全 × 联合—个体方差分解融合（ADMM + 块坐标下降求解）；在低维因子上以时空双重去偏 Lasso 配合因果森林估计气候→产量因果效应；以多流稀疏 CUSUM / INSPECT 结合 PCMCI 做在线变点预警。
创新点： 1) 异构-多模态张量稀疏融合统计学习框架，拟证明非渐近收敛率与变量选择一致性；2) 时空双重去偏 Lasso × 因果森林联合估计，拟证明 √(NT) 一致渐近正态；3) 多流在线高维变点检测与因果对接预警，拟证明 ARL/EDD 上界。
进展： 申请中（2026 年 6 月填表，研究期 2 年），尚无实验结果。计划实证粒度为新疆 14 地州 87 县 2010—2024 年、约 1 305 个"县 × 年"样本。本人主笔撰写申报书与配套综述。
意义： 目标是填补"高维多模态统计推断 + 棉花产量 + 气候风险预警"这一组合的空白。诚实边界：以上全部为拟开展工作，三个定理是目标而非结果；与气象信息中心、兵团统计局的数据合作仅达成初步意向，尚未签署。

[14] Apple Origin Traceability via Machine Learning & Transfer Learning

[14] 基于机器学习与迁移学习的苹果溯源模型研究

National国家级 Ongoing在研 Participant参与  ·  2025 – Present今  ·  202510758022

Background: Cross-year verification of declared apple origin lacks an honest public benchmark, and existing NIR traceability studies are widely affected by evaluation leakage.
Method: Near-infrared diffuse-reflectance spectra with Savitzky-Golay smoothing, SNV and cubic-spline interpolation onto a common 229-band grid; a leave-one-harvest-year-out (LOEO-Year) protocol built on a fold-restricted encoder with strict source-domain z-scoring; benchmarked against nearest centroid, PLS-DA, RBF-SVM, vanilla ProtoNet, 1D CNN and 1D ResNet.
Innovation: 1) builds a nine-domain cross-year apple NIR benchmark; 2) proposes a leakage-controlled leave-one-year protocol keeping target-year information entirely out of feature learning; 3) quantifies systematically, for the first time in the apple NIR literature, how severe evaluation leakage is.
Status: Ongoing, mid-term review passed. I lead-authored the proposal and the field data collection across Xinjiang, Shandong and Gansu. At K=5 the shared-encoder protocol reports macro F1 = 0.931 against 0.665 under the leakage-controlled protocol, a gap of 0.266 widening to 0.333 on the hardest 2025 fold.
Significance: Establishes an honest evaluation standard for cross-year origin tracing and gives practical guidance for multi-face field scanning. Note: the project PI of record is a teammate; my role is participant, though I am first author on the resulting paper.

背景： 商用苹果宣称产地的跨年份鉴别缺少诚实的公开基准，已有近红外溯源研究普遍存在评测泄漏。
方法： 近红外漫反射光谱经 Savitzky–Golay 平滑、SNV 与三次样条插值统一到 229 波段；设计"折受限编码器 + 源域严格 z-score"的留一年（LOEO-Year）评测协议；与最近质心、PLS-DA、RBF-SVM、vanilla ProtoNet 及 1D CNN / 1D ResNet 对比。
创新点： 1) 构建九域跨年苹果近红外基准数据集；2) 提出泄漏受控的留一年评测协议，使目标年信息完全不进入特征学习；3) 首次在苹果近红外文献中系统量化评测泄漏的严重程度。
进展： 在研，已通过中期检查。本人主笔申报书并牵头新疆、山东、甘肃的田间数据采集。K=5 时共享编码器协议三折平均 macro F1 = 0.931，而泄漏受控协议仅 0.665，差距 0.266；在最难的 2025 折扩大到 0.333。
意义： 为跨年苹果溯源确立诚实的评测标准，并为田间多面扫描协议给出实践指导。说明：项目立项负责人为团队同学，本人为参与成员，但为所产出论文的第一作者。

[12] Apple SSC Prediction via Functional Data + Image & Spectral Feature Fusion

[12] 函数型数据视角下融合图像信息和光谱特征的苹果 SSC 预测模型研究

Provincial省部级 Excellent优秀结项 Participant参与  ·  2024 – 2026  ·  S202410758069

Background: Soluble solids content is the key quality index for apples, yet Vis-NIR models routinely treat a spectrum as a discrete vector and discard its continuity, and lack a composite way to compare competing models.
Method: Converts discrete spectra to functional data on a B-spline basis, takes image colour features as scalar covariates, and fits a semi-functional partial linear regression model; Boruta screens the colour features and a three-dimensional DISO system over R², MAE and RMSE scores the models.
Innovation: 1) builds a semi-functional partial linear model for apple SSC from the joint perspective of functional data and feature fusion; 2) proposes the three-dimensional DISO evaluation system for quantitative model comparison; 3) assembles an interpretable pipeline of colour-feature extraction, functional fusion and composite evaluation.
Status: Completed. 552 Aksu Fuji apples, 36 colour features screened to 26 by Boruta, 21 competing models. The best model reaches R² = 0.915, MAE = 0.464, RMSE = 0.592 and ranks first over 200 repeated runs. Output: one paper in Spectroscopy and Spectral Analysis, on which I am fourth author; the project PI is a teammate.
